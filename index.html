MeshWeave Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026

Canonical copy: vibealive.us/privacy/meshweave.html.

MeshWeave is a live Starlink constellation globe for iPhone and iPad. It plots public spacecraft element sets on a 3D Earth and can optionally lock onto a Starlink user terminal on your local network. It is free. There are no in-app purchases.

What MeshWeave collects

MeshWeave does not create an account. It does not run ads, analytics SDKs, or tracking.

Precise location is used only if you turn on This dish and grant Apple’s When In Use permission. Coordinates stay on this device to place your terminal on the globe when the dish does not publish GPS. They are never uploaded, sold, or used for advertising. Turning dish tracking off stops using location.

Local network is used only to read GPS and look angle from a Starlink dish at the standard terminal address on your LAN. That traffic does not leave your network.

Catalog and maps

Satellite positions are loaded from public CelesTrak element sets (Supplemental GP / GP). Gateway markers come from a public community list of earth stations. Those requests do not include your location or Apple ID.

Permissions

  • Location (When In Use, precise) — optional dish pin if the terminal has no GPS. Deny it and the globe still works.
  • Local network — optional, only to talk to a dish on this LAN.
  • Photos (add only) — optional, only if you tap Capture.

Third parties

  • CelesTrak for public orbital element sets
  • Community gateway listings (FCC IBFS / public datasets)
  • Apple (App Store, Core Location, local network)

MeshWeave does not sell personal data. It is not an official SpaceX, Starlink, or xAI product.
